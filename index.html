    How this page works

    • Today's top 10 is the latest sweep's list, the same one the morning message links: finds that are new or cheaper today first, best ranked first, then the best still on sale with your size in stock; at most two per brand and four per store.
    • The page holds the best 500 finds, ranked by discount, quality, what you open, refundability, your size in stock and your favourite brands, with caps per category and per brand so no single label floods it.
    • Three times a day (early morning, late morning, late afternoon, US Eastern) a Python script on GitHub reads each store's sale listings directly, applies your sizes and rules, checks fabric and stock from the store's own product data, removes sold-out finds and rebuilds this page. No AI model reads pages or decides anything. Watchlist brands are checked on every run, and a short report goes out each morning. A find no store listing has confirmed for two days is hidden; shorts and short sleeves stay out of the top 10 and the alerts from November to March.
    • In stock means a size in your range showed as available on the store's page or product data. Check size means the store hides per-size stock (Mr Porter, YOOX, Nordstrom Rack and Farfetch shoes, among others).
    • Sold out? hides a find right away; the next sweep deletes it. More like this and Hide teach the ranking directly: hides apply instantly, everything else at the next sweep. Undo them in Settings.
    • Also at lists other stores selling the same piece; the cheapest one is shown. Was-price looks inflated means this store's original price is well above what other stores list for the same item, so the discount is measured against their price.
    • Highest end means a top-rated maker (or a top technical brand), the piece rated Very good or better, an original price in the top 40% for its category, and at least 50% off.
    • Leather: new leather, suede and shearling are left out (new dress shoes and boots are assumed leather unless the listing says otherwise). Secondhand leather is fine, so leather shoe makers show up from eBay and Poshmark.
    • Secondhand discounts are measured against the typical original price for that brand and category, since resale listings rarely show retail.
    • Quality combines the brand's reputation for construction and fabrics, the materials named in the listing and customer reviews. Hover a chip to see why.
    • Pound, euro and Canadian prices are converted at the rates in Settings; duties aren't included.
